Turn the room into an ambient score.

RESONA listens to the room, the audience and the musicians, and turns them into an experimental ambient pad that follows the improvisation in real time. No two performances sound the same, because no two rooms do.

What it does

An instrument that plays the space it's in.

Plug in a few microphones, point them at the stage and the audience, and RESONA builds slow harmony, pulses and texture out of what it hears.

01

Several inputs at once

Add as many USB microphones or audio interfaces as you have, or loop an audio file. Each input gets its own gain, pan and on/off.

02

Harmony that follows the room

Pitch detection hears the notes being played and steers slow, diatonic chord changes toward them, in the key and scale you choose.

03

A constellation, not a mixer

Every part of the synth is a light. Drag around a node to change two parameters at once, with as many fingers as you like.

04

Distance is time

Move nodes closer or further apart to set each engine's rhythm, how much each microphone is heard and how far back the grains reach.

05

Arpeggio by scale degree

Pick which degrees of the current chord the arpeggio uses, over one to three octaves. It stays quiet in silence and opens up when people play.

06

Grains of the room

Short fragments of the last few seconds of live sound are pitched to the scale and woven back into the pad.

Two screens

One to play, one to project.

Screen 01

Performer

The full constellation with labels, values and settings. Press Space to fold the bar away and keep only the lights.

Screen 02

Projector

A second window that mirrors the constellation in real time, without any text: slow mist, travelling pulses and an aurora that keeps changing. Drag it to the projector and go fullscreen.

The deriva node

Four worlds in the corners of the screen.

Place the deriva node near a corner to give the whole synth that character; the centre is clean. Set a direction and speed on its ring and a light drifts out toward the edge and back, carrying the sound through the worlds it crosses.

Made for the stage

Built for improvised concerts.

RESONA was made to sit next to the musicians and react to them: microphones on the stage and in the audience, the constellation on the performer's screen, the lights on the wall behind. Everything is processed locally, so it works without a network in the venue.

  • Echo cancellation when you play through speakers, clean input on headphones or an isolated PA
  • Layout, inputs and settings are remembered for the next show
  • Calm visuals: slow mist and a living aurora, meant for meditation as much as for concerts

Get RESONA

Install it, or just open it.

Same instrument either way. The desktop app runs in its own window and never opens a local server.

A regular installer with a Start menu entry and a desktop shortcut. Works offline.

Run it in the browser

No install. RESONA runs on the Web Audio API in any recent browser, on a laptop or a tablet.

  • Open resona.html and allow the microphone
  • Use your browser's "Install app" to keep it in its own window
  • Works offline after the first load
